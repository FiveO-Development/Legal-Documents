# Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy ("Policy") describes how **FiveO Development | OfficerMills** ("Bot Owner," "we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, and protects information in connection with the following Discord bots (collectively, the **"Bots"**):

- **Reviewly Feedback**  
- **Post A Note**  
- **FiveO Jail Bot**  
- **NixQueue**  
- Any additional public bots released by the Bot Owner in the future  

By using any of the Bots, you ("User") agree to the terms of this Policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect
The Bots may collect and process the following types of information:  
- **Discord User Data**: User IDs, usernames, and server membership information as required for bot functionality.  
- **Server Data**: Channel IDs, message content, and role information if directly relevant to the operation of bot features (e.g., reviews, notes, queue management).  
- **Usage Data**: Commands issued to the Bots, timestamps, and event logs for debugging and performance purposes.  

We do **not** collect sensitive personal information (e.g., passwords, payment data, government IDs).

---

## 2. How We Use Information
Information collected by the Bots is used solely to:  
- Provide and improve bot functionality  
- Ensure compliance with Discord’s Terms of Service and Community Guidelines  
- Debug errors and maintain reliability  
- Prevent abuse or misuse of the Bots  

---

## 3. Data Sharing and Disclosure
We do not sell, rent, or trade User or Server data.  
Data may be shared only in the following limited circumstances:  
- **Legal Compliance**: If required by applicable law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request.  
- **Discord**: As necessary to comply with Discord’s API policies or enforcement actions.  

---

## 4. Data Retention
We retain collected data only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Policy.  
Temporary logs and event data may be stored for debugging but are periodically deleted.  

---

## 5. Data Security
We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure.  
However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.  

---

## 6. User Rights
Users may request deletion of their data or opt out of certain data collection by contacting the Bot Owner.  
Requests can be made by opening an issue on the relevant GitHub repository or contacting us directly via Discord (**OfficerMills**).  

---

## 7. Children’s Privacy
The Bots are not directed at children under the age of 13.  
Users under 13 are not permitted to use the Bots in accordance with Discord’s Terms of Service.  

---

## 8. Modifications to This Policy
We may update this Policy from time to time.  
Any changes will be posted publicly on GitHub, and continued use of the Bots after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated Policy.  

---

## 9. Governing Law
This Policy shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the **State of Tennessee, United States**, without regard to its conflict of law principles.
